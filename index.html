FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Here are answers to some of the questions pet parents ask us most often.

Do I need an appointment? +

We recommend appointments for routine visits, vaccinations and consultations. Please call us if your pet needs urgent attention.

What should I bring to my first visit? +

Bring any previous medical records, vaccination information and a list of medications your pet is currently taking.

Do you treat emergencies? +

We provide urgent veterinary assessments during clinic hours. For life-threatening emergencies, please contact an emergency veterinary hospital immediately.

How often should my pet have a checkup? +

Most pets benefit from at least one wellness examination each year. Senior pets or animals with chronic conditions may benefit from more frequent visits.

Do you see cats and dogs? +

Yes. We provide comprehensive veterinary services for dogs and cats. Contact our team if you have a different type of companion animal.
